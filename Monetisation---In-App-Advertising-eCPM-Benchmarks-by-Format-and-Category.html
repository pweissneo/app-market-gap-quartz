Monetisation - In-App Advertising eCPM Benchmarks by Format and Category

eCPM (effective cost per mille) benchmarks for in-app advertising across formats, categories, and platforms. iOS commands a 2.5x CPM premium over Android, rewarded ads dominate on eCPM but interstitial is the volume leader, and non-gaming categories offer higher ARPPU than most gaming genres.

Revenue Timing: IAA vs IAP vs IAS

The most important dimension for eCPM optimization is understanding how revenue accumulates over the user lifetime — not just raw eCPM.

Revenue StreamDay 1 % of Day 60Day 7 % of Day 60Key Insight
IAA (In-App Advertising)57%89%Most front-loaded; eCPM-heavy early
IAP (In-App Purchase)38%60%Moderately back-loaded
IAS (In-App Subscription)28%52%Most back-loaded; requires 90-day view

Source: AppsFlyer State of App Monetization 2026

Day 7 IAA LTV reliability: Day 7 IAA revenue is a reliable proxy for Day 60 — it captures 89% of the total. This means IAA monetization can be evaluated and optimized much faster than IAP or IAS.

Gaming-specific variation:

  • Hypercasual is most front-loaded: 63% of revenue on Day 1 (reflects extreme early churn)
  • Casino is most back-loaded for IAP: only 23% of Day 60 revenue arrives on Day 1 (players deliberate before spending)

eCPM Optimization Levers

  1. Platform-specific waterfall (20–40% improvement): Separate iOS and Android waterfall configurations. iOS: AppLovin first ($5 CPM avg). Android: Google AdMob anchor + AppLovin + Mintegral. Most small publishers use identical configs for both platforms — the gap is large.
  2. Floor price strategy: iOS — aggressive floors (3–5CPMbanner,15+ rewarded video). Android — moderate floors (1–2CPMbanner,8–10 rewarded) to maintain fill rate in emerging markets.
  3. Frequency cap: iOS — 1 interstitial per 3–4 minutes. Android — 1 per 5–6 minutes. iOS users more sensitive to disruption; Android users more receptive but longer sessions compensate.
  4. Rewarded ads as engagement loop anchor: The rewarded ad is not just a revenue format — it is a retention mechanic. When the reward aligns with the app’s core value action (completing a habit, winning a game level), it reinforces the behavior rather than interrupting it. Gameloft Asphalt 8: 20% higher ARPDAU with rewarded ads embedded in the gameplay loop.

Gap Implications

For Category - Habit Trackers

No major habit tracker currently embeds rewarded ads into the habit completion loop. This is the single largest eCPM×UX opportunity in habit app monetization. A habit tracker where watching a short ad “waters” a virtual plant (gamification) or extends streak protection captures rewarded ad eCPMs (8–30)whilereinforcingthecorehabitloop.AverageDay90IAAARPUforcasualgamesis0.55 — a well-designed habit app with rewarded integration could approach or exceed this benchmark.

For Category - Journaling Apps

Privacy-sensitive context limits ad targeting effectiveness and creates user trust risk. eCPMs for native and rewarded are highest ($3–30), but banner and interstitial formats risk breaking the therapeutic cognitive state. Contextual rewarded ads (after writing, not during) with zero-knowledge targeting are the only viable ad format in this category.
