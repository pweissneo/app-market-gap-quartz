Hypothesis - Chronic Kidney Disease Progression Tracking No App Monitors GFR Decline And Predicts Dialysis Need

37 million Americans have CKD but most have no idea their GFR is declining until they hit kidney failure. “I was in stage 2 and did not know it — by stage 4 I was suddenly facing dialysis with no preparation.” No app monitors GFR decline rate and predicts when you will need dialysis or a transplant. Your kidneys are failing — and you are flying blind.

The Five Core Pains

1. Silent Progression Tracking Failure

The failure:

  • “My GFR dropped from 60 to 45 over 2 years and I had no idea until the doctor told me at my annual physical”
  • CKD is silent until late stages
  • No consumer-facing way to track GFR decline between doctor visits
  • Annual lab work is insufficient for meaningful tracking

The reality:

  • “My GFR dropped from 60 to 45 over 2 years and I had no idea until the doctor told me at my annual physical”
  • “I did not even know what GFR was until my doctor told me mine was declining”
  • “Stage 2 felt fine - I had no symptoms - but my kidneys were already losing function”
  • “There is no app that shows you your GFR trend over time and says ‘your kidney function is declining at this rate‘“

2. Dialysis Preparation Timing

The failure:

  • “I had 6 months notice that dialysis was coming but I had no app to help me prepare for what that means”
  • Dialysis preparation is overwhelming and under-supported
  • No guidance on what to expect, how to prepare, what lifestyle changes matter
  • Patients face dialysis with no preparation

The reality:

  • “I had 6 months notice that dialysis was coming but I had no app to help me prepare for what that means”
  • “I did not know what dialysis was like until I started it - no one told me what to expect”
  • “There is no app that says ‘your kidney function will likely require dialysis in 8 months - here is how to prepare’”
  • “The transition to dialysis was brutal because I had no time to adjust my diet, lifestyle, or expectations”

3. Transplant List Navigation

The failure:

  • “I am on the transplant list but I have no way to track where I am or what I can do to move up”
  • Transplant list status is opaque
  • Patients do not know what determines their position
  • No guidance on what actions might help

The reality:

  • “I am on the transplant list but I have no way to track where I am or what I can do to move up”
  • “I have been waiting 3 years and I have no idea if my position has changed”
  • “There is no app that shows you your transplant list status and what factors determine your ranking”
  • “I wanted to do everything I could to stay healthy enough for a transplant but I had no guidance”

4. Stage-Specific Guidance Absence

The failure:

  • “I am in stage 3 — there should be an app that tells me exactly what I should be doing at each stage to slow progression”
  • Each CKD stage requires different management
  • No app provides stage-specific guidance
  • Patients are left to figure out what matters

The reality:

  • “I am in stage 3 - there should be an app that tells me exactly what I should be doing at each stage to slow progression”
  • “Stage 3 means I need to watch my protein intake, stay hydrated, avoid certain medications - but how do I track any of this”
  • “There is no app that says ‘at stage 3 CKD you should be doing X, Y, Z to slow progression’”
  • “Diet, exercise, medications, monitoring - it all changes as you progress but there is no guide”

5. Care Coordination Fragmentation

The failure:

  • “My nephrologist and my primary care doctor do not communicate — I am the only one tracking the full picture”
  • Multiple doctors, no coordination
  • Patients carry the burden of being their own care coordinator
  • Critical information falls through the cracks

The reality:

  • “My nephrologist and my primary care doctor do not communicate - I am the only one tracking the full picture”
  • “My primary care did not know about my kidney function decline until the nephrologist sent a letter months later”
  • “I am tracking my GFR trends, my medications, my diet changes - no one else is doing it”
  • “There is no app that bridges the communication gap between my multiple specialists”
