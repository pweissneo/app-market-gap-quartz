Category - Small Business CRM Apps

Mobile CRM apps let sales teams manage contacts, leads, and deals on the go. The best mobile CRM apps offer features nearly equivalent to desktop versions, enabling real-time data access and seamless sales operations from anywhere. The market ranges from free options for small teams to enterprise-grade solutions at $25+/user per month.

Market Gaps and Weaknesses

Reported Weaknesses

  1. HubSpot: Big price jump from Starter to Professional; no mobile calling in free plan
  2. Bitrix24: Mid-level learning curve; requires third-party integration for business card scanning; lacks marketing tools
  3. Zoho: Clunky mobile interface in certain modules; mobile emailing requires separate Zoho Mail app subscription
  4. Salesforce: No free plan; annual billing only; limited marketing and project management on mobile
  5. Capsule: Only one-way Google Contacts integration; lacks email tracking and templates in free plan
  6. Pipedrive: Project management locked in higher-priced Power plan; email sync locked in Advanced plan
  7. ClickUp: Not a traditional CRM; requires custom setup; tough navigation with many modules
  8. monday CRM: No sales analytics on mobile; no collaboration tools like document sharing on app
  9. Insightly: No mobile chat; no AI on mobile; lacks sales automation
  10. EngageBay: Marketing automation only in Growth plan ($59.79/user); AI limited to chatbot and email marketing

Identified Gaps

  1. No single app excels at all: Each app has significant weaknesses in specific areas
  2. Free plans are limited: Most free plans cap users at 2-15, limiting adoption for growing teams
  3. AI features uneven: Only Zoho, Salesforce, and Capsule have meaningful AI on mobile
  4. Offline functionality limited: Most apps require connectivity; few offer robust offline mode
  5. Integration complexity: Third-party integrations often required for basic features (business cards, email)
