Hypothesis - Contact Lens Prescription Renewal And Supply Coordination No App Manages Prescription Renewal And Contact Lens Supply Together

45M contact lens wearers in the US. Contact lens prescriptions expire in 1-2 years depending on state. Online lens ordering exists but is disconnected from prescription management. Existing lens tracker apps only track wearing, not supply or renewal. No app manages prescription renewal AND contact lens supply together because prescription apps and lens ordering apps are separate. Prescription apps track your prescription, lens ordering apps let you buy lenses, lens tracker apps remind you to wear them — but none of them talk to each other. “I ran out of lenses and my prescription expired and I spent 3 hours trying to figure out what to do.”

The Five Core Pains

1. Prescription Renewal Is Separate From Ordering

The failure:

  • Prescription renewal is a separate process from ordering lenses
  • Online renewal exists but is not connected to ordering
  • “I renewed my prescription but forgot to order lenses”

The reality:

  • Prescription verification and lens ordering should be one workflow
  • Existing apps split these across separate services
  • No unified app that says “renew → order → ship”

2. Annual Supply Calculation

The failure:

  • How many lenses do you actually need per year?
  • No app calculates this for your specific prescription
  • You guess and either run short or overorder

The reality:

  • Daily lenses, bi-weekly, monthly all have different supply needs
  • Eye exam is once a year, supply is supposed to last a year
  • “You need 730 lenses for daily wear for a year” — no app tells you this

3. Auto-Reorder When Low

The failure:

  • Your supply runs out but you have no idea until the day-of
  • “I have 2 weeks of lenses left” — nobody tells you this in advance
  • Emergency lens orders are expensive and slow

The reality:

  • Current apps track wearing, not supply remaining
  • “LensAlarm” and “Contact Lenses Tracker” exist but only for wearing reminders
  • Supply monitoring and automatic reordering is not solved

4. Wearing Tracking Disconnected From Prescription Status

The failure:

  • Lens tracker apps track wearing but not prescription status
  • “I have been wearing my lenses for 6 months but my prescription is for bi-weekly”

The reality:

  • No connection between wearing data and prescription validity
  • Wearing beyond prescribed replacement schedule is a health risk
  • No app correlates the two to flag compliance issues

5. Prescription Expiry Catching You

The failure:

  • Prescription expires, you cannot order, scramble for renewal
  • “I did not realize my prescription expired until I tried to order”
  • Some users wear expired prescriptions because renewal is hard

The reality:

  • Contact lens prescriptions are valid 1-2 years depending on state
  • FTC Contact Lens Rule requires prescribers to give prescriptions automatically
  • Renewal can happen online but process is fragmented
  • “I wore my lenses for 2 weeks past expiry because I could not figure out renewal”
