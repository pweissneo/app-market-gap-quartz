The Six Core Pains

1. Claim Filing

The failure:

  • “My bag is lost, how do I file?”
  • No streamlined mobile claim
  • Airline websites are confusing

What should exist:

  • “One-tap claim” — file in seconds
  • Airline auto-detect: “You’re on Delta: file claim directly from boarding pass.”
  • PIR form auto-fill from airline account
  • Photo upload for bag tag and receipt
  • Status tracking with claim number

2. Real-Time Bag Tracking

The failure:

  • “Where is my bag right now?”
  • No real-time lost bag tracker beyond airline apps
  • Users must call the airline

What should exist:

  • Cross-airline bag location timeline
  • ETA prediction for delivery
  • Alert when bag is found

3. Compensation Calculator

The failure:

  • “How much am I owed?”
  • No compensation calculator exists
  • Montreal Convention, DOT, EU261 rules are complex

What should exist:

  • Automatic jurisdiction detection (DOT vs EU261 vs Montreal Convention)
  • Compensation ceiling calculation per region
  • Delay vs permanent loss differentiation
  • Depreciation calculator for bag value

4. Documentation Management

The failure:

  • “What receipts do I need?”
  • No document organizer
  • Receipts and forms easily lost

What should exist:

  • Auto-capture from email (PIR confirmation, etc.)
  • Required docs checklist with deadlines
  • Expiration alerts for filing windows
  • PDF export for insurance or credit card claims

5. Multi-Airline History

The failure:

  • “Filed with 3 airlines this year”
  • No multi-airline claim history
  • No pattern detection

What should exist:

  • Centralized claim history across all airlines
  • Airline performance comparison
  • Credit card coverage tracking

6. Reimbursement Tracking

The failure:

  • “Did I get paid yet?”
  • No payment tracker
  • Users forget to follow up

What should exist:

  • Payment status monitoring
  • Follow-up reminders when delayed
  • Settlement history
