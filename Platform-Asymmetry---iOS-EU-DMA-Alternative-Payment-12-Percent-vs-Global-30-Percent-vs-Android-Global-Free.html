Platform Asymmetry — iOS EU DMA Alternative Payment 12% vs Global 30% vs Android Global Free

The Asymmetry

iOS in the EU (under DMA) now offers a non-IAP payment path with fees as low as 12% (2% initial acquisition + 5% Store Services + 5% CTC, without tier 2 services) — versus the standard 30% IAP fee for all other regions and for EU developers who stay on IAP.

Android globally has no equivalent platform fee restriction. Developers can use any payment processor, and Google takes 15-30% on IAP but the Play Store’s payment policy is far less restrictive than Apple’s. There is no EU-specific regulatory asymmetry creating a dramatically lower alternative payment tier.

The asymmetry is therefore three-way:

PlatformStandard IAP FeeCheapest Alt-Payment PathConditions
iOS (non-EU global)30%30% (IAP only)No alternative exists
iOS (EU, with AEUTA + no tier 2)17-20%12%Must forgo App Store discovery, reviews, auto-updates, Analytics
Android (global)15-30%~0% (external processor)No regulatory-mandated alternative; standard Play Store policies

Which Side Benefits

Android benefits for apps that want to use alternative payment processors outside a narrow set of EU-eligible developers. iOS still holds the discovery advantage (App Store search, reviews, featuring) but Android has a globally open payment landscape with no equivalent ATT or regulatory gatekeeping.

Within iOS, the EU’s DMA-created 12% tier benefits large, self-distributed apps (Fortnite, Spotify) that can survive without App Store discovery. Smaller developers in the Small Business Program still find the 10% alt-payment path more expensive than staying on 15% IAP.

Gap It Opens

This creates a platform-asymmetry window for B2B SaaS billing infrastructure apps and payment aggregator apps targeting EU iOS developers:

  1. Developer tooling for EU alt-payment compliance: Apps that help developers navigate the AEUTA terms, calculate effective fees across different configurations, or manage the “either IAP or alt-payment” constraint.
  2. EU-only indie developer arbitrage: An indie developer building an app exclusively for EU users could use alt-payment at 12% and build their own discovery channel (social, newsletter, web) — a viable model on iOS that has no equivalent for Android developers who already had this freedom globally.
  3. Payment processor differentiation: Stripe, Paddle, and others have positioned for this gap, but niche aggregators focusing specifically on the EU iOS alt-payment flow (with the scare sheet UX, the tier 2 service tradeoffs, and the quarterly opt-in cycle) remain undersupplied.

Who Could Exploit It

  • Payment aggregator startups targeting EU iOS developers with flat-rate processing + compliance tooling, bypassing Apple’s IAP while absorbing the 12% fee.
  • Developer SaaS tools that model fee optimization across iOS/EU vs iOS/global vs Android configurations.
  • App discovery platforms that fill the void left when developers opt out of tier 2 App Store Services (no App Store discovery → need alternative discovery channels).
