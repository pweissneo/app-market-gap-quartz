Hypothesis - Daily Routine Consistency Tracking No App Measures Whether Your Daily Routines Are Actually Consistent

People build daily routines — morning routine, workout, meditation — but do not measure whether those routines are actually consistent. “I have been doing this for 3 weeks” is not the same as “I have been doing this for 6 months.” No app tracks routine consistency over time — they only show whether you did the routine today. “Consistency is the only thing that matters for habit formation” but there is no way to know if you are being consistent. You should be able to say “I am 73pct consistent with my morning routine this quarter” but no app gives you that.

The Five Core Pains

1. Routine Drift Invisibility

The failure:

  • Your routines slowly break down and you do not notice
  • You start strong but over weeks and months the routine weakens
  • “I used to do my morning routine every day but now I do it sometimes”

The reality:

  • “I did my workout 4 times this week but I used to do it 6 times — that is a regression”
  • “My meditation practice went from 30 minutes to 10 minutes without me noticing”
  • “The routine is still there but I am doing it with less commitment”
  • No app alerts you that your routine consistency is declining

2. Streak Obsession

The failure:

  • Apps track streaks but do not measure if the routine is actually consistent
  • A “30-day streak” can hide 8 missed days and 22 partial days
  • Streaks reward showing up, not consistency of quality or effort

The reality:

  • “My streak is 30 days but I actually only completed 20 of those days fully”
  • “The streak metric is misleading — I have been doing the routine but half-heartedly”
  • “A streak does not tell you if the routine is getting stronger or weaker”
  • “I have a streak but I do not know if I am actually being consistent”

3. Quality vs Quantity

The failure:

  • Doing something 365 days in a row at 50pct quality vs 200 days at 90pct
  • Streaks count both the same but one is clearly better
  • “I technically worked out every day but for 5 minutes instead of 45”

The reality:

  • “I showed up but I did not do the work — should that count as consistency?”
  • “I meditated every day but for 30 seconds — that is not a consistent practice”
  • “Consistency should be about depth, not just frequency”
  • No app distinguishes between “doing something” and “doing it well”

4. Routine Decay Patterns

The failure:

  • No visibility into when and why your routines fall apart
  • You do not know which routines are most likely to decay
  • “Why do my workout routines always fall apart after 6 weeks?”

The reality:

  • “I can see I am in a routine decay period but I do not know why”
  • “Weekends are when my routines break down — but I have no data to prove it”
  • “Every year in January I start strong but by March I am back to nothing”
  • “I need to see the pattern to fix the pattern”

5. No Consistency Score

The failure:

  • Should be able to say “I am 73pct consistent with my morning routine this quarter”
  • No app gives you a consistency score, only a streak count
  • You cannot compare your consistency across different routines

The reality:

  • “My morning routine consistency this quarter: unknown”
  • “Am I more consistent with my workout or my meditation? I have no idea”
  • “How am I doing compared to last quarter? No way to know”
  • “I want a score, not a streak — tell me how consistent I actually am”
