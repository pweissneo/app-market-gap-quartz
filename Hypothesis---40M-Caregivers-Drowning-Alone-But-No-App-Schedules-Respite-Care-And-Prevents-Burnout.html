Hypothesis - 40M Caregivers Drowning Alone But No App Schedules Respite Care And Prevents Burnout

63M+ Americans provide caregiving. $1.01T value of unpaid caregiving annually. Only 14% of caregivers receive respite, but 38% say they need it. Respite care — taking breaks so caregivers can rest — is the #1 thing caregivers say they need but can’t get. “When can I take a break?” “No one else helps.” “I’m drowning.” Most caregivers do this alone, with no support, no scheduling help, and no one to take over. Current apps are general care coordination (CaringBridge, AARP app) or scheduling tools — none focus specifically on respite scheduling, burnout detection, and caregiver self-care. The gap: no app that schedules respite care, coordinates the care team, detects burnout early, tracks caregiver self-care, provides emergency backup, or tracks caregiving financial costs.

The Six Core Pains

1. Respite Scheduling

The failure: No app schedules respite care. No one to take over. What should exist: Respite scheduler, relief requests, volunteer matching, professional respite search, Medicaid respite search, calendar blocking.

2. Care Team Coordination

The failure: Multiple family members, no app coordinates care team for respite. What should exist: Care team coordinator, task assignment, skills matching, pitch-in reminders, family accountability.

3. Burnout Detection

The failure: Physical, emotional exhaustion; no app detects burnout early. What should exist: Burnout quiz, pattern detection, alert system, prevention tips.

4. Self-Care Tracking

The failure: Caregivers neglect themselves; no app tracks self-care. What should exist: Self-care goals, me-time logging, health tracking, accountability.

5. Emergency Backup

The failure: Sudden needs, last-minute; no app provides emergency backup. What should exist: Emergency contacts, quick request, response tracking, backup network.

6. Financial Tracking

The failure: Caregiving is expensive; no app tracks costs. What should exist: Expense logging, time value tracking, coverage options, Medicaid respite eligibility, tax deductions.
