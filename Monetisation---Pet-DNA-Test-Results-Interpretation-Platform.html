The revenue:

Why it works:

  • 72% of owners are confused and willing to pay for clarity
  • Monthly subscription provides ongoing value as research evolves
  • High-margin recurring revenue from a one-time test purchase

2. One-Time Genetics Consultation — $199

The revenue:

  • 45-minute call with a certified pet genetic counselor
  • Personalized action plan based on your pet’s specific results
  • Breed-specific guidance from experts
  • Q&A session to address specific concerns
  • Written summary and follow-up resources

Why it works:

  • Pet parents want human guidance on complex decisions
  • One-time purchase for those who want depth without subscription
  • High perceived value for personalized expert time

3. Breed Identification Kit — $49

The revenue:

  • Budget option for curious pet parents
  • Basic breed breakdown
  • Simple health alerts
  • Core temperament insights
  • Getting started guide

Why it works:

  • Lower barrier to entry for price-sensitive customers
  • Upsell path to premium interpretation
  • Volume play at a lower price point

4. Health Screening Add-On — $89

The revenue:

  • Detailed health risk breakdown
  • Breed-specific condition risks
  • Actionable prevention recommendations
  • Monitoring schedule for at-risk conditions
  • Vet discussion guide with specific talking points

  • One-time add-on at checkout increases average order value
  • Directly addresses the “what do I do with this information” problem

5. Ancestry Expanded Report — $149

The revenue:

  • Full family tree and breed history
  • Extended family matching with other tested pets
  • Breed heritage storytelling
  • Genetic diversity insights
  • Hereditary trait explanations

Why it works:

  • Ancestry is emotionally compelling and shareable
  • Pet parents love learning about their pet’s background
  • One-time purchase with high emotional value

6. Veterinary B2B White-Label — $299/MONTH

The revenue:

  • Veterinarians offer interpretation to their clients
  • Branded portal for the veterinary practice
  • Client reports generated automatically
  • Revenue share on referrals
  • CE credit access for veterinary staff

Why it works:

  • Veterinarians are too busy to provide thorough DNA interpretation
  • Creates distribution through trusted veterinary channels
  • Recurring B2B revenue scales with network effect
