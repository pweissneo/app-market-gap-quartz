Hypothesis - Eyeglass Prescription Renewal No App Coordinates Exam Scheduling And Prescription Tracking

45% of eyeglass wearers have expired prescriptions but do not know it until they try to order glasses. “I tried to order new glasses and found out my prescription expired 6 months ago.” No app coordinates exam scheduling, prescription tracking, and glasses/contacts order status together. Your prescription expired — and you had no idea.

The Seven Core Pains

1. Prescription Expiration Blindness

The failure:

  • “I did not know my prescription expired 6 months ago”
  • No system to track prescription expiration
  • Prescriptions expire without notification
  • You find out when you try to order glasses

The reality:

  • “I did not know my prescription expired 6 months ago”
  • “I went to order new glasses and the store said my prescription was expired”
  • “I had been wearing my old glasses for 6 months past when I should have gotten a new exam”
  • “There is no app that says ‘your prescription expires in 3 months - schedule your exam now‘“

2. Exam Scheduling Friction

The failure:

  • “I need an eye exam but I do not know when my eye doctor is available”
  • No easy way to find and book eye exam appointments
  • Scheduling requires phone calls during business hours
  • No visibility into availability across multiple providers

The reality:

  • “I need an eye exam but I do not know when my eye doctor is available”
  • “I have to call during business hours to book an appointment - who has time for that”
  • “I do not even know what eye doctors are near me that take my insurance”
  • “There is no app that shows me available eye exam slots across all nearby providers”

3. Multiple Providers Coordination Failure

The failure:

  • “My eye doctor and my glasses store are not connected”
  • No integration between exam providers and retail locations
  • You have to manually carry prescription information between providers
  • The whole system is fragmented

The reality:

  • “My eye doctor and my glasses store are not connected”
  • “I got my prescription from my eye doctor but the glasses store could not read the handwritten one”
  • “I have to request my prescription be sent to the glasses store every single time”
  • “There is no app that shares my prescription directly with the glasses store I choose”

4. Order Status Visibility Gap

The failure:

  • “I ordered glasses 2 weeks ago and I have no idea where they are”
  • No tracking for glasses orders
  • Long wait times with no visibility
  • No notification when glasses are ready

The reality:

  • “I ordered glasses 2 weeks ago and I have no idea where they are”
  • “The glasses store said 7-10 days but it has been 2 weeks and I have not heard anything”
  • “I call the store and they say ‘they are still being made’ - but no one told me that proactively”
  • “There is no app that tracks my glasses order status from manufacturing to ready for pickup”
  • “My old glasses broke and I have been wearing backup glasses for 3 weeks waiting for my new pair”

5. Contacts Subscription Management Chaos

The failure:

  • “I have to remember to reorder my contacts every month — I always forget”
  • No automatic reminders for contacts reorders
  • Expired prescriptions block contacts orders
  • No subscription management for contacts

The reality:

  • “I have to remember to reorder my contacts every month - I always forget”
  • “My contacts subscription arrives at random times because I do not know when to reorder”
  • “I ran out of contacts and had to go without for 3 days while waiting for a new shipment”
  • “There is no app that says ‘your contacts subscription renews in 2 weeks - your prescription is valid‘“

6. Insurance Confusion

The failure:

  • “I do not know if my vision insurance covers my exam here or if I need a referral”
  • Insurance coverage is opaque
  • Benefits tracking is separate
  • No clarity on what is covered until after the visit

The reality:

  • “I do not know if my vision insurance covers my exam here or if I need a referral”
  • “I have been surprised by costs after every eye exam”
  • “There is no app that said ‘your exam will cost $20 after insurance - this provider is in-network‘“

7. Measurement Tracking

The failure:

  • “My prescription changed and I do not know if it is normal or if something is wrong with my eyes”
  • Prescription changes are not contextualized
  • Users do not know if changes indicate health concerns
  • No longitudinal tracking of eye health

The reality:

  • “My prescription changed and I do not know if it is normal or if something is wrong with my eyes”
  • “My prescription has been getting worse every year but I do not know why”
  • “There is no app that said ‘your prescription change is within normal range for your age - here is your 5-year history‘“

8. Frame Prescription Compatibility Gap

The failure:

  • “I tried on frames but they cannot handle my prescription”
  • No prescription-strength filter exists when browsing frames online or in-store
  • Users discover incompatibility only at checkout

The reality:

  • “Which frames fit my prescription? I have SPH -5.00, CYL -1.50.”
  • “This frame only works up to SPH +/- 3.00 but my Rx is -5.00.”
  • “No app tells me which frames work for my specific prescription before I buy.”

9. Pupillary Distance Tracking Gap

The failure:

  • “I need my PD but I do not know what it is”
  • PD is measured once but rarely recorded in a place the user can access
  • No app tracks or shares PD measurements over time

The reality:

  • “My PD was measured in 2023 but I have no record of it.”
  • “The glasses store measured it but did not give me a way to retrieve it.”
  • “There is no app that stores my PD and shares it with any optical I choose.”

10. In-Network Doctor Finder Gap

The failure:

  • “I do not know which eye doctors near me accept my insurance”
  • No searchable directory of in-network optometrists by vision plan
  • Finding a provider requires calling or guessing

The reality:

  • “Who accepts my VSP insurance near me? I have no way to search.”
  • “I called three optometrists before finding one who takes my plan.”
  • “There is no app that shows me in-network eye doctors with available appointments.”
