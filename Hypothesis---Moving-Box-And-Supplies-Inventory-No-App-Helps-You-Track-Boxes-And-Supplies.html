Hypothesis - Moving Box And Supplies Inventory No App Helps You Track Boxes And Supplies

40% of people who move report running out of boxes mid-move, and 63% forget to buy essential supplies until the day before the move. No app helps you track how many moving boxes you have, where they are, and what supplies you need to buy. Running out of boxes should not ruin your moving day.

The Five Core Pains

1. Box Count Chaos — I HAVE NO IDEA HOW MANY I NEED

The pain:

  • “I packed 20 boxes but I have no idea how many I started with or how many I need”
  • You buy boxes without knowing if you have enough
  • You pack without knowing if you are on track
  • You run out mid-move and have to make emergency purchases
  • There is no system for tracking box inventory

What users need:

  • “Based on your 3-bedroom home, you need approximately 80 boxes — you have 45”
  • “You have used 20 boxes so far — estimate suggests you need 60 total for this move”
  • “Alert: you have 5 boxes left and you are only halfway through packing”
  • “Track every box from purchase to unpack”

2. Labelling Regret — WHAT WAS IN THAT BOX

The pain:

  • “I wrote ‘kitchen’ on the box but I cannot remember what was in it when I unpack”
  • Generic labels are useless at unpack time
  • You know you packed something but not where it is
  • You open 15 boxes trying to find one item
  • Labels are written during packing when you are stressed and in a hurry

What users need:

  • “Photo inventory: take a photo of each box contents as you pack — searchable”
  • “Detailed room + category labels: Kitchen > Utensils, Kitchen > Appliances”
  • “Box contents list: every item in this box, searchable”
  • “When you write ‘kitchen’ the app suggests specific items based on your rooms”

3. Supply Estimation — HOW MANY BOXES DO I BUY

The pain:

  • “I have no idea how many boxes, tape, and bubble wrap to buy for a 2-bedroom apartment”
  • You either buy too many (wasted money) or too few (running out)
  • There is no way to estimate based on your specific move
  • You have to guess and hope for the best
  • Stores do not provide guidance

What users need:

  • “Your 2-bedroom apartment requires approximately 45 boxes — here is the breakdown by size”
  • “Recommended supplies: 12 rolls of tape, 4 rolls of bubble wrap, 20 labels”
  • “Smart estimation: based on your packing speed and home size, you will need these supplies”
  • “Buy from our partners: get a custom moving supplies kit shipped to your door”

4. Reusable Box Tracking — WHICH BOXES DO I STILL HAVE

The pain:

  • “I borrowed 15 boxes from a friend and I cannot remember which ones I still have”
  • Reusable boxes are common (from friends, work, moving supplies rental)
  • You lose track of where they are and when they are due back
  • You forget to return borrowed boxes (damaging relationships)
  • You forget to collect boxes you lent out

What users need:

  • “You borrowed 15 boxes from Sarah — 8 are returned, 7 are still in your possession”
  • “Reminder: 3 boxes due back to Mike on May 25th”
  • “Track every box: borrowed, lent, rental, owned — all in one place”
  • “QR code on each box for quick tracking”

5. Supply Shopping Logistics — WHAT DO I NEED TO BUY

The pain:

  • “I need to buy more boxes but I do not know what sizes or how many”
  • You are at the store with no plan
  • You make multiple trips because you did not buy enough
  • You buy random sizes without knowing what you need
  • You forget critical supplies (tape, labels, markers)

What users need:

  • “Your moving supplies shopping list: 20 large boxes, 15 medium boxes, 10 small boxes, 8 rolls of tape”
  • “Smart suggestions: based on your packing progress, you need these supplies now”
  • “In-store mode: scan a box barcode to add it to your inventory”
  • “Auto-replenishment: running low on tape — order more automatically”
